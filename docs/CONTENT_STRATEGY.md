# Content strategy (3 posts / week, YouTube Shorts + Instagram Reels)

## Positioning
A broad personal brand — "a young engineer figuring out life in Bangalore, learning in public" — rather than a
pure finance channel. Four pillars:
💰 Money · 💪 Fitness & health · 💻 Tech career · 🏙️ City life

Same handle on both platforms. Bio pattern: "SWE in Bangalore | money, fitness & life in my 20s | learning in
public, not financial advice". Keep the employer unnamed and check your employer's social-media / outside-activity policy.

## What tends to work (2026 roundups; directional, not guaranteed)
- Hook with a specific ₹ outcome or number in the first 2 seconds.
- On-screen text that carries the story (many viewers watch muted).
- Comparisons ("X vs Y — which actually wins?"), personal journeys, one lesson per video.
- Shareability (DM sends) and watch time are the strongest reach signals; reply to comments.
- Originality: no other-app watermarks; upload natively.
- Formats last weeks, trending audio days — build around formats.

## Weekly rhythm
| Day | Slot | Pillar rotation |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | talking head (main) | Money |
| Wed | talking head or faceless HyperFrames explainer | Fitness / Tech career |
| Fri | talking head | City life / personal story |

## Video template (matches styles/finance_story_style.json)
0–3 s: number hook · 3–6 s: question · body: 3 numbered points with serif-accent headers + icon badges ·
last 3 s: payoff number / summary card. 45–90 s.

## Idea bank
- What ₹X/month actually buys in Bangalore (rent, food, travel breakdown)
- What fitness really costs me per month (gym, protein, food)
- 3 things nobody tells you in your first tech job
- IPO vs SIP — what I learned as a beginner (personal experience, not advice)
- PG vs flat: the real monthly difference
- My payday routine, step by step
- Weekend in Bangalore under ₹1,000

## Automation boundaries
Automatable: ideas/scripts (LLM), editing (this repo), titles/captions/hashtags (LLM), scheduling (Meta Business
Suite, YouTube Studio). Human: recording, replying to comments. API auto-posting is possible later (Instagram Graph
API needs a Creator/Business account + Facebook Page + Meta app; new YouTube Data API projects upload private-only
until Google's audit).
